Shipping & FFL Policy

Firearm Shipping Guidelines (FFL Required)

Federal law strictly prohibits shipping firearms directly to home addresses. Firearms ship only to licensed Federal Firearms License (FFL) dealers.

StepAction Required

  1. 1

    Step 1: Select FFL

    Select or specify a licensed local FFL dealer during checkout.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Submit License

    Ensure dealer emails a copy of their signed FFL license to ffl@aumakuadefense.com with order number.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Shipping

    We verify the license and ship directly to the FFL dealer’s physical location.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Transfer & Pickup

    Complete ATF Form 4473, pass background check, pay dealer transfer fee, and take possession.

State Restrictions & Carrier Requirements

We do not ship restricted items (e.g., high-capacity magazines or banned firearms) to restricted jurisdictions. Firearms require adult signature upon delivery to the FFL. Ammunition and firearms cannot ship to P.O. Boxes.

Failed Transfers

Inspect firearms BEFORE completing Form 4473. If the background check fails or transfer is refused, return shipping costs and a 15%–20% restocking fee will be deducted from your refund.

Refund & Return Policy

Firearms & Ammunition

  • Firearms: All sales are FINAL once transferred from the FFL dealer. Inspect firearm prior to completing Form 4473. Defect claims post-transfer must go through manufacturer warranty.
  • Ammunition & NFA Items: All sales of ammunition, suppressors, and NFA items are strictly FINAL and non-returnable.

Apparel, Knives & Accessories

  • 30-Day Window: Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery for brand new, unworn, unwashed apparel, and unused/unsharpened knives in original packaging.
  • Process: Email info@aumakuadefense.com for an RMA number prior to return shipment. Return shipping costs are customer responsibility unless item was defective or sent in error.
