# Image credits

The photographs in `assets/photos/` are resized WebP crops of licensed stock. They are example images until the client supplies photography. No AI images were used.

The home hero uses the lava photograph on both parallax layers. The Hawaiian ridge original is 3264×1840, which cannot cover a portrait tablet hero at 1.5× without upscaling, so the hero uses lava instead. Ridge stays on the brand story and About. The gear-up band uses the same lava files as the hero. Desktop scenic files are 1440, 1920, and 2880 pixels wide. `lava-phone.webp` is 1300×1688 and `lava-tablet.webp` is 1800×1688, both at the full source height of the 3000×1688 original and not upscaled. `hawaii-ridge-phone.webp` is 1300×1840.

Gear cards use 640 and 1280 pixel files. The 960-wide step is omitted so a tablet-width layout selects 1280.
