Meet our founder

Josh Kaikala

Born in Hāna, Maui, Josh Kaikala was raised off-grid during his earliest years, establishing a deep-rooted, lifelong connection to his Hawaiian heritage and the land. Though his journey eventually led him to be anchored in the Arizona desert, the values of loyalty, self-reliance, respect for community, and an unyielding connection to his roots remain central to his identity.

Aumakua Defense was born from Josh’s personal passion for the industry and a deep-seated commitment to family. In Hawaiian tradition, an ‘aumakua is an ancestral guardian spirit—an ever-present protector of the family. For Josh, this isn’t just a brand name; it is a personal pledge.

Supported by his ‘ohana (family), Josh founded Aumakua Defense to ensure that every individual and family has access to reliable, high-precision tools designed to protect what matters most.

“Protection isn’t just a business for us, it’s a family duty. Every firearm, blade, and piece of gear we select is chosen with the same standard of care and reliability I demand for my own family.”

— Josh Kaikala, Founder

We invite you to explore our curated selection of firearms, blades, apparel, and tactical gear—crafted and selected to protect what matters most to you.
