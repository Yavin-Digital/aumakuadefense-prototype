Last Updated: 11/01/2026

At Aumakua Defense, accessible from www.AumakuaDefense.com, we honor the Hawaiian tradition of the ‘aumakua—an ever-present ancestral guardian spirit. Grounded in our core values of family and protection, we treat the privacy and security of your personal information with the utmost respect and care.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, purchase our products (including firearms, knives, accessories, and apparel), or interact with our customer support services.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification Information: Contact data (full name, billing address, shipping address, email, phone), government/compliance ID (date of birth, driver’s license/ID required for age verification, background checks, or FFL transfers), and payment card details (processed securely via third-party payment gateways).
  • Order and FFL Transfer Data: FFL dealer name, address, license number, and transaction history required for legal and tax compliance.
  • Automatically Collected Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages viewed, time spent, and cookies/tracking technologies.

How We Use Your Information

  • Fulfill orders, process transactions, manage payments, ship merchandise, and facilitate required FFL transfers.
  • Verify age and legal eligibility in compliance with federal, state, and local laws.
  • Provide customer support, process returns, and send order updates.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud, and comply with regulatory recordkeeping (e.g., ATF compliance).
  • Send marketing communications (with opt-out availability at any time).

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with trusted service providers (payment processors, shipping carriers), designated FFL dealers, legal/regulatory authorities when required by law, or as part of a business transfer/acquisition.

Age Restrictions (Minors)

Our website is not directed toward children under 18 years of age (or under 21 for regulated firearm purchases). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors under 18.

Data Security & Rights

We implement SSL encryption and standard security measures to protect your data. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data (subject to legal recordkeeping duties) by contacting support.
