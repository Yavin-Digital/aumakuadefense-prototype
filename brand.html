Color palette

Copper numbers are measured from the lockup PNG. Military green, ink, and paper are proposed. The olive wall samples come from the build brief’s description of Mike’s mockup. That mockup file is not in this repo, and those olives are not room colors.

#C07A5A --accent

Copper mid. Measured luminance median (p50) of opaque warm pixels in the copper lockup. Buttons and 1px jewelry. Ink text on this fill clears WCAG AA (about 5.2:1).

#673A27 --copper-shadow

Oxide. Measured dark copper (near p08). White logo on this field is about 9.5:1. Also the link color on paper.

#DF9A73 --copper-highlight

Measured p75. Text and links on military green (about 5.6:1). Mid copper as small text on green does not clear AA.

#F7BA96 --copper-bright

Measured p90. Highlight reference and focus outline. Not a room color.

#2C3324 --brand Proposed

Military green. Deep olive drab, darker than the mockup wall, so paper and copper highlight clear AA. Needs Mike’s sign-off. Not sampled from a pixel in the logo (the files are transparent).

#1A1916 --ink Proposed

Gunmetal. Text on copper buttons and on paper. Needs Mike’s sign-off.

#F4F0E8 --paper Proposed

Warm paper for text on green and for this guide. Needs Mike’s sign-off.

#7D7359 Not a room

Brief sample of the mockup olive wall. Copper mid on this is about 1.4:1. Do not use it behind the mark or behind text.

#8A795D Not a room

Second brief sample of the mockup wall. Same restriction. Reference only.

The brief’s starting copper samples were about #B87350 mid, #5B362D shadow, and #E0A784 highlight. The PNG measures slightly lighter at the median. This guide uses the file measurement. Those brief figures stay here as the approximation, not as a second palette.

Typography

Voice and tone

The only published lines are the name and the tagline. Everything else on the prototype is marked draft or client to supply. This voice is proposed.

Sound like

  • Direct, short, and physical.
  • The tagline, unchanged: “Where Ancient Protection Meets Modern Precision.”
  • Name the business Aumakua Defense, as published.
  • Say when a price, photo, or policy is missing.

Leave out

  • Invented prices, reviews, years, certifications, or stats.
  • FFL, shipping, or legality claims.
  • Hawaiian words the client has not supplied. The name itself is fine.
  • The live meta line “expert defense services.” That page is a coming-soon placeholder.
  • Emoji. Use the line icons.

Usage rules

Do

  • Use the five PNG files. Keep them transparent and unaltered.
  • Put copper on military green or ink. Put white on oxide or military green.
  • Keep military green as the room. Use copper for buttons, 1px lines, and the mark.
  • Mark proposed colors and proposed type until Mike signs off.
  • Leave clear space around the lockup at least the cap-height of the wordmark PNG. This clear-space rule is proposed.

Do not

  • Recolor, redraw, outline, or set the mark in type.
  • Use the rough SVG traces.
  • Place the copper mark on #7D7359 or #8A795D.
  • Set #C07A5A as small text on paper or on military green. Use oxide on paper, and copper highlight on green.
  • Paint a room in copper. Copper is jewelry, not wall paint.
  • Add tiki, hibiscus, leis, or other clip-art. A thin weave divider, echoing the band in the mark, is the only pattern.
