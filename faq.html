Firearm Purchases & FFL Transfers

How do I buy a firearm online?

Add firearm to cart, select an FFL dealer during checkout, have dealer send FFL license to us, track shipment, and complete Form 4473 background check at dealer pickup.

What is an FFL dealer fee?

Dealers charge an independent fee (50 average) to conduct the background check and log the transfer. Paid directly to dealer.

Orders & Shipping

Do you ship to restricted states?

We enforce all federal, state, and local laws. Orders with restricted items (e.g., magazine limits) destined for restricted areas will be canceled.

How long does shipping take?

Accessories/apparel ship in 1–3 business days (3–7 days transit). Firearms ship within 1–3 business days after FFL license verification.

Returns & Brand Story

What does ‘Aumakua’ mean?

An ‘aumakua is a traditional Hawaiian ancestral guardian spirit. Aumakua Defense provides modern precision tools honoring that spirit of protection.
